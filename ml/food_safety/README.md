# USDA: 
# Raw ground meats, poultry, seafood and variety meats (liver, tongue, chitterlings, etc.), should be used or frozen within 1 to 2 days after purchase.
# Raw beef, veal, lamb and pork roasts, steaks and chops should be used or frozen within 3 to 5 days.
# After cooking, meat, poultry and seafood can be safely stored in the refrigerator 3 to 4 days.
